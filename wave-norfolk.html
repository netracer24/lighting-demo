About your lighting assistant

Your lighting assistant is an AI system run by S&S Production Services, LLC. It turns what you ask for into cues and looks on your console.

  • It can misunderstand a request or get a look wrong. Check every change before it's used in a service.
  • Use Service Lock during services; the assistant can't change the console while it's on.
  • The S&S team set it up and support it. Tap Help any time to reach a person.
